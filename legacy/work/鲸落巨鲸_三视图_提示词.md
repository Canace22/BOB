## 一、主体提取

**主体**：鲸落巨鲸——一具被海洋生态完全覆盖的巨型须鲸尸体

被保留的主体特征：
- 蓝灰色皮肤褶皱，脊背与体侧长满白色、紫色珊瑚群落与海藻、海葵丛
- 躯干中部有**城堡状钙化生态构造**从体表长出（不是人造石头建筑，是珊瑚/生态堆积）
- 躯体内部透出青绿与琥珀色**生物荧光**，透过伤口与半透明膜窗外泄
- 尾柄与尾鳍被漂流海草和珊瑚壳覆盖
- 体侧一道长斜向旧伤痕（可作为跨区域地标）
- 解剖约束（沿用项目设定）：张口内是**须板**不是牙齿；头背**成对喷气孔**；尾鳍**水平**

**已排除**（属于原画环境，不进三视图）：
水母 · 鱼群 · 蝠鲼 · 潜水员 · 海底岩石 · 阳光射线 · 水体光斑 · 大气雾

## 二、提示词（英文，直接投给图像模型）

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Character/prop production design sheet — ORTHOGRAPHIC THREE-VIEW TURNAROUND of ONE colossal whale-fall baleen whale, the main subject extracted from a supplied deep-sea fantasy illustration.

Subject: a single colossal deceased baleen whale (fictional species) whose entire body has been colonized by a thriving marine ecosystem. Blue-gray skin ridges; dense white and purple coral colonies, kelp strands and anemone tufts overgrowing the back, flanks and tail; one ruined castle-like ecological structure of calcified coral rising from the mid-trunk body surface (grown from the body, not man-made stone architecture); soft teal-and-amber bioluminescent light glowing from inside the carcass through wounds and translucent membrane windows; tail stock and horizontal flukes crusted with drifting weed and coral. One long diagonal old scar along a flank. Hanging flat bristled BALEEN visible in the open mouth, never teeth; paired blowholes on the dorsal head; HORIZONTAL tail flukes. Fictional whale ecology.

Layout: one clean production design sheet on warm-gray paper, wide landscape, THREE views of the SAME individual at consistent scale, all in flat ORTHOGRAPHIC projection with NO perspective, NO foreshortening, NO camera angle, NO dramatic lighting:
(1) left of sheet — SIDE VIEW, full lateral profile, head facing LEFT, tail to the right, entire silhouette inside frame, baleen and flukes readable;
(2) right of sheet — FRONT VIEW, head-on, showing open mouth with baleen, paired blowholes and the symmetrical body width;
(3) bottom-center — TOP VIEW (dorsal / plan), spine running along the vertical axis, flukes at one end, coral growth read as a clean outline.
Thin neutral warm-gray guide rails and small unlabeled alignment ticks only.

Style: sophisticated hand-painted gouache with precise contours, clear graphic silhouettes, matte tactile surfaces, muted underwater palette — ink-blue skin, chalk-white barnacles, teal and purple coral, restrained amber internal glow. Even, neutral studio lighting that keeps every silhouette readable; large quiet dark background areas.

Strictly exclude: jellyfish, fish schools, manta ray, scuba diver, seabed rocks, sun rays, water caustics, atmospheric haze, any second creature, human figures or buildings. Subject only. NO text, NO numbers, NO labels, NO watermark.
```

## 三、微调提示

| 想改什么 | 改哪里 |
| --- | --- |
| 改成「正/侧/**背**」三视图 | 把 `(3) bottom-center — TOP VIEW` 换成 `BACK VIEW`，并删掉 dorsal/spine 那句 |
| 单开一张侧视图（不用拼版） | 只保留 `(1) SIDE VIEW` 段，其余两视图段落删除 |
| 出图后要标注尺寸 | 加：`add a thin scale bar with tick marks, no numerals`（或干脆后处理加字） |
| 想弱化「城堡」感 | 把 `castle-like ecological structure` 改成 `mounded calcified coral ridge` |
| 想保留原画那种深海底色 | 把背景从 `warm-gray paper` 改成 `deep navy abyssal background` |
